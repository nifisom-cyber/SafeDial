About SafeDial

SafeDial is a simple yet powerful solution that provides instant access to emergency services when you need them most.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to make asking for help faster and simpler, so that anyone can reach police or an ambulance in a few taps, even when they are scared or in a hurry.

OUR VISION

Our vision is a South Africa where no one hesitates to ask for help because they don't know how.

OUR VALUES

Frequently asked questions

What is SafeDial?

SafeDial is a fictional emergency app made for a training project. It shows how short codes could give people quick access to help.

What do the emergency codes do?

Each code, such as *1# for police, is an example of a one-step shortcut to a type of help. They are placeholders and do not connect to real services.

Social Media Campaign

A one-week awareness pilot for the SafeDial concept, with every post leading to a relevant page section.

Audience and channels

South African adults and caregivers interested in personal safety planning. Use Instagram and Facebook for visual explainers, with WhatsApp status for short community reminders.

Content and cadence

Publish three feed posts per week, supported by brief Stories and one community reminder. Rotate demo explainers, trusted-contact planning, and official emergency information.

Voice and safeguards

Use calm, clear language. Label SafeDial as a fictional concept; never imply its sample codes connect to responders or replace official emergency numbers.

Success measures

Review reach, saves, shares, link clicks, and contact-page visits at week end. Add campaign tracking and consent controls before any live promotion.
